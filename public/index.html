What not to do

Do not take a 12–18 month re-skill — an SDE pivot, a heavy certification track. Rupees you can earn back; the training hours and the recovery you spend at 26 you cannot. A higher-paying job with worse hours is a net loss against the October 2027 date, however good the offer looks. Optimise the job for schedule control first, salary second.
